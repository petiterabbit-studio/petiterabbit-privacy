# Privacy Policy

PetiteRabbit Watch Faces respects your privacy.

This application does not collect, store, or share personal information.

The watch face may display information provided by Wear OS system services and complications, such as:

- Time and date
- Battery level
- Step count
- Heart rate
- Weather information

This data is processed locally on the user's device and is not transmitted to PetiteRabbit or any third party.

This app does not use advertising SDKs or third-party analytics services.

If you contact us by email, your email address will only be used to respond to your inquiry.
